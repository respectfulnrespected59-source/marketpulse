# The 5 Rules

### 1 · Small probes, defined risk
Never bet the pot. **Most probes lose — that's expected.** Size every entry so a
total loss can't wreck you (the app defaults to a **20%-of-pot** probe budget).
Survival first.

### 2 · DCA-tilt in — don't chase green candles
Buy in **pieces over time**, leaning heavier when the engine reads the name as
*cheap*, lighter when it's *hot*. You're averaging in, not timing tops. The DCA
Wizard shows plain vs lump vs signal-tilt so the trade-offs are honest.

### 3 · Escalate winners, cut melters — never revenge-size
Add only to what's **working**. A cratered probe is a small, planned loss — log it
and move on. Doubling down to "get it back" is how pots die.

### 4 · A basket, not one lottery ticket
Spread across several AI names (the app's **AI names** preset is your sandbox —
*apply the method to it; it is NOT a buy list*). One blow-up should be a scratch,
not a knockout.

### 5 · Receipts + time
Run it over a **3–6 month** horizon. Track every play — win or lose — in the Live
Tracker and Pot Tracker. Let the real numbers steer the next move.

# The Math of Survival

You do **not** need to be right most of the time. With defined-risk probes:

| Cap each loss at | Let winners run to |
|---|---|
| your probe size (**1R**) | **2R, 3R, or a 5R+ runner** |

Then even a **35–45% hit-rate** comes out ahead, because the winners are bigger
than the losers. That's **positive expectancy** — a statistical edge over many
plays, **not** a promise on any single one. Some months will be red. That's the game.

> *Expectancy, plain: (win% × avg win) − (loss% × avg loss). Keep avg win bigger
> than avg loss and you can be wrong more than half the time and still grow.
> Blow your risk cap once and the math breaks — that's why Rule 1 is Rule 1.*
