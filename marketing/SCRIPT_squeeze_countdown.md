**VISUAL:** Manim. Bollinger bands (cyan) and Keltner channels (grey dashed)
drawn as two nested envelopes. The Bollinger pair animates inward.

**QUANTUS:**
> A squeeze is simple. Two envelopes around price. The Bollinger bands measure
> how far price is actually swinging. The Keltner channel measures how far it
