FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is MarketPulse?

A trading research app with a transparent signal engine, an options chain with IV and Greeks, a DCA wizard, a probe sizer, paper trading, and Proof Mode backtests. It reads the market and shows its reasoning. You decide what to do.

Does it trade for me?

Today it trades on paper only. Real-money execution is being built on your own brokerage account: every trade waits for your approval by default, and full auto is an opt-in switch with hard daily limits, a kill switch, and a loss circuit breaker.

Do you ever hold my money?

Never. When real-money trading ships, you connect your own brokerage account. Your money stays with your broker, and you can revoke access any time.

Will it make me money?

Nobody can promise that, and anyone who does is selling you something. Our own backtests show the engine losing to simply holding in a bull run and beating it in a choppy market. What MarketPulse does promise is transparency and risk caps so one bad trade can't end your pot.

Is paper trading the same as real trading?

No. A paper trade never touches the market. Real trades face slippage, fills, and your own nerves. That's why every result here is labeled for what it is.

What markets does it cover?

US stocks and ETFs, major crypto, US options chains, and South African (JSE) names. Prices come from free delayed feeds, so a reading can be up to a minute old.

What does it cost?

The research app is free. Pro tiers add real-money execution as it ships, monthly or lifetime. See pricing above.
